Test the model on the UR Fall dataset (leave-one-clip-out)

Each clip is labelled from its name (fall-xx → 1, adl-xx → 0). Landmarks go through preprocessing (median fills missing body points and smooths noise, mode picks the majority posture in each short window). The thresholds are then learned on 18 clips and tested on the clip that was left out, repeated for all 19 clips.

Student prototype. It reports a possible fall only, does not diagnose injury and is not a certified medical or emergency system. Dataset: UR Fall Detection Dataset (Kwolek & Kepski, 2014).
